# MONSERA — World Bible

> Canon reference for setting, peoples, factions, places and characters. Names are provisional.
> Originality rule: no element may be a renamed copy of an existing franchise's creature, race,
> place or character. When in doubt, change it.

## 1. Tone

**Cozy on the surface, serious underneath.** Warm pastoral life, humour and small-town gossip, with a
war that brings real consequences: rationing, displacement, loss. Violence is never glorified, and the
game respects the player's choice to stay a civilian. Kith are not "pets that fight": they are partners
with their own will, and in the world, kith labour and kith soldiering are debated issues.

## 2. The world: Sera

A temperate continent in its early industrial age. Railways and steam ferries are recent. Electricity
exists in cities, powered by **Spark-aspect kith** and **leystones** (crystallised earth-energy).
Rural life still runs on muscle, water wheels and kith help. Ancient ruins from the **Kilnwright era**
(a vanished civilisation that first bonded kith) are everywhere.

### 2.1 Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Kith** | Creatures of Sera, from mouse-sized to house-sized. Intelligent, but not speaking. (Plural: kith.) |
| **Bond** | The mutual link between a person and a kith. It is formed with a **Bond Charm**, a woven token that the kith has to accept. Forced bonds are illegal in the Compact and common in the Crown's army. |
| **Aspect** | The elemental nature of a kith: Verdant, Tide, Ember, Stone, Gale, Spark. |
| **Leystone** | Crystallised aspect energy found in ruins and mountains. It is the fuel behind the Crown's industry. |
| **The Greying** | A blight that has spread through northern farmland for ten years. Soil turns ash-grey and crops fail. It is the root cause of the war. |
| **Marks** | Common currency (Crown-minted, used everywhere). |

## 3. Peoples

| People | Home | Identity | Relationship with kith |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Humans** | Everywhere; majority in the Crown and the vales | Adaptable, industrious, divided by politics | Wide: farm partners, workers, soldiers |
| **Rootkin** | Thornwood forests (southwest) | A slow-lived people whose skin grows bark with age. They go into a winter dormancy called "the Hush" and grow living architecture from trees. Decisions are made by consensus circles. Memory is sacred: elders keep oral histories in song. | Kith are kin. Bonds are lifelong and never forced. Verdant and Gale kith are sacred. |
| **Cindral** | Emberreach highlands (northeast), under-mountain forge-halls | A heat-resistant people with ember-veined skin and short stature. They are glassmakers, smelters and weather-readers. Guild-houses are led by matriarchs, and oaths are recorded in coloured glass. | Working partnership: Stone and Ember kith in mines and forges. Many guilds are contracted to the Crown. |
| **Brinari** | Salt coasts and the Tidehouse islands (south and east) | An amphibious people with gill-lines and webbed hands. They are sailors, merchants and cartographers. Ship-guilds are democratic, and contracts are sacred. | Tide and Gale kith pull ships and carry mail. Brinari are officially neutral, but they trade with both sides. |

Guideline: cultures differ in **values, architecture, food, kith ethics and speech**, not only in
looks. Avoid "elf/dwarf" shorthand in art or writing.

## 4. Factions and the conflict

| Faction | Seat | Wants | Why they are sympathetic | Why they are dangerous |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Aurelian Crown** | Aurel, northern capital (humans, allied Cindral guilds) | Farmland and kith labour to feed Greying-stricken cities; railway expansion; leystone | Its people are starving. It brings rail, medicine and schools. | Conscription, forced bonds, annexation dressed up as "protection" |
| **Thornwood Compact** | Circle of Hollins, deep Thornwood (Rootkin plus free human towns) | Sovereignty; stopping leystone mining, which they believe spreads the Greying | Defends its homes and a sustainable way of life | Isolationist, slow, uses guerrilla tactics that endanger civilians; hides a truth about the Greying |
| **Brinari Tidehouses** | Port Saltwhistle | Profit and free seas | Honest brokers, shelter refugees | Sell to both sides; would trade the valley for a good contract |
| **Lowmere Vale Charter** | Brambleford (the player's home) | To stay neutral | Ordinary people | Too small to stay neutral for long |

**The central mystery (late game):** the Greying is caused by broken Kilnwright leystone networks.
Crown mining makes it worse, but the Compact has known about a partial cure and kept it hidden.
Resolution requires cooperation, not the conquest of one side by the other. (This is the main-story
spine; details are still open.)

## 5. The vertical-slice region: Lowmere Vale

A green, river-cut valley between the Crown's northern plains and the Thornwood. The new
**Aurel–Saltwhistle railway** runs through it. The valley is protected only by the old **Vale Charter**,
which both powers have signed.

| Location | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wrenfield** | Player's farm | An abandoned railway waystation farm. The previous owner (the Wren family) left when the Greying rumours started. It has a farmhouse, 6–10 plots at first, a broken paddock and a well. |
| **Brambleford** | Village hub | Station, general store, Kith Warden's lodge, tavern (the Crooked Lantern), Reeve's hall, militia post. |
| **Brambleford Crossing** | Tactical battlefield | A stone bridge over the Lowmere river with fords, hedges, a mill and a hill. It is the chokepoint between the Crown road and the village. |
| **Whisperwood** | Exploration area | A forest east of the farm. It has paths, a log-blocked trail, mud flats and berry thickets. |
| **Mirelight Pond** | Kith habitat | A glowing pond inside Whisperwood. Tide and Verdant kith live here, and the pond glows at night. |
| **The Sunken Kiln** | Small dungeon | A half-buried Kilnwright workshop with 3–5 rooms. Its mechanic is heat vents and pressure plates. A guardian (Obelith) watches over it. The reward is a Kilnwright Lantern (lights dark areas) and lore. |

## 7. Calendar

- 4 seasons of 28 days: **Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter**. The year number is shown.
- 7-day week (provisional names): **Moonday, Tideday, Rootday, Emberday, Galeday, Starday, Restday**.
- A day runs from 6:00 to 2:00. Festivals are one per season (post-slice).

## 8. Naming guide

- Kith names: two syllables, English-rooted compounds or diminutives (Sprigmole, Rillet, Loamox).
  Never combine a real animal with a well-known franchise suffix or naming pattern.
- Place names: English rural (-ford, -field, -mere, -wood) for the vale; Rootkin names are soft
  consonants (Hollins, Ysvaren); Cindral names are hard consonants (Karrak-Deep); Brinari names are
  sibilant and maritime (Saltwhistle, Sessary).
