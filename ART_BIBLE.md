# MONSERA — Art Bible

> Visual rules for everyone producing art. Current state: **placeholder only** (generated by
> `tools/generate_placeholder_art.gd`). Final style is decided with a single style-test map before
> any mass production (Phase 9–10).

## 1. Target

**2D HD pixel art, top-down (¾ view, Stardew-like perspective).** Crisp pixel sprites at native
resolution, combined with modern effects: smooth camera, soft lighting, day/night tint, particles, and
subtle screen-space glow. Everything is readable first and beautiful second.

## 2. Technical canvas

| Item | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Internal resolution | 640×360, integer-scaled (×2 = 720p, ×3 = 1080p, ×4 = 1440p, ×6 = 4K) |
| Tile | 16×16 px |
| Player / NPC | 16×24 px body frame (fits a 16×32 cell with headroom), origin at the feet |
| Kith (overworld) | S 16×16, M 24×24, L 32×32, XL 48×48 |
| Kith (battle) | 64×64 (S/M), 96×96 (L/XL) with a front and a back pose |
| Tactical units | 16×16 map icons on 16 px cells + 48×48 portraits |
| Filtering | Nearest; no mipmaps; no sub-pixel sprite scaling |
| Palette | Free palettes (no hardware limit), but **one master palette** of about 64 colours for coherence |
| Outlines | 1 px darker-hue outlines on characters and kith (not pure black); no outlines on terrain |
| Light direction | Top-left |

## 3. Mood by context

| Context | Palette | Mood |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farm and village | Warm greens, honey yellows, soft browns, sky blue | Cozy and safe |
| Forest and habitats | Deep teals, moss, bioluminescent accents (Mirelight Pond) | Wonder |
| Ruins and dungeons | Desaturated stone and ember orange, Kilnwright glyph cyan | Mystery |
| Crown military | Steel grey, navy, brass; hard geometric shapes | Order and threat |
| Compact | Bark browns, living green, woven textures; organic shapes | Resilience |
| Tactical battlefield | Slightly desaturated terrain, high-contrast unit team colours | Clarity |

Day/night: the global tint (`DayNightTint`) handles time of day. Art is drawn for **noon light**.
Night light sources (windows, lanterns, glowing kith) are separate emissive layers.

## 4. Kith visual rules

1. The silhouette must be recognisable in solid black at overworld size.
2. Aspect is shown by **a feature**, not only by colour (a sapling, a smouldering crest, a quartz shell).
3. Big readable eyes or a clear "face" direction; expressive idle animation (breathing, blinking,
   the quirk of that kith).
4. Matured forms keep 2–3 features of the base form.
5. Forbidden: designs that could be mistaken for any existing franchise's creature. Check every
   concept against this rule before pixel work begins.

## 5. Tactical readability

- Team colour on a base disc under each unit and a coloured border on portraits (blue player,
  red Crown, green Compact, gold neutral).
- Terrain types must be distinguishable in greyscale (value contrast): plain, road, forest, hill,
  ford/shallow water, deep water, bridge, building, fortification.
- Overlays: movement (blue, 40 % opacity), attack (red), support range (green), danger zone (hatched
  red). Always visible above terrain, below units.
- Health: strength pips (1–10) on the unit, as a number and not only as a bar.

## 6. UI

- Warm parchment panels with wood/brass trim for life-sim UI; a cooler steel variant for military UI.
- Pixel font, 8 px (small) / 16 px (normal) at internal resolution. Until chosen, the Godot default font is a placeholder.
- The HUD is minimal: clock and date (top right), money (under clock), hotbar (bottom), prompts (bottom centre).
- Prompts show the actually bound input (keyboard key now; controller glyphs later).

## 7. Animation

| Asset | Frames (minimum) |
| --- | --- |
| Player walk | 4 directions × 4 frames, 8 fps |
| Player tool use | 4 directions × 3–4 frames per tool |
| NPC walk | 4 directions × 4 frames |
| Kith overworld | idle 2–4, walk 4 per direction (left/right can mirror) |
| Kith battle | idle loop 4–6, attack 3–5, hit 2 |
| Crops | one sprite per growth stage + watered soil variant |

### Farming readability (lesson from Phase 3)

- A mature crop must be recognisable at a glance and from the tile above. Use a distinct silhouette
  plus a "ready" cue (produce visible above the soil, a colour pop, a subtle idle sway). The Phase 3
  placeholders fail this: mature and growing crops look too similar.
- Watered vs dry soil must differ in value (dark vs light), not only in hue.

## 8. Placeholder policy

- Placeholders live in `assets/placeholder/` and are **generated** by
  `tools/generate_placeholder_art.gd`. Do not hand-edit them. Change the generator.
- Placeholders use flat colours and simple shapes, never ripped or traced art.
- Replacing a placeholder must not require code changes (same size, same path via the scene/tileset).

## 9. Originality and legal rules

- No assets, sprites, tiles, fonts, music or sounds from Pokémon or any other commercial game.
- Third-party assets only with a compatible licence (CC0, CC-BY with credit in `CREDITS.md`).
- Everything else is made for MONSERA.

## 10. Style-test checklist (before mass production)

- [ ] One 40×23-tile farm screen in final style, at day and night
- [ ] Player + 1 NPC walk cycles
- [ ] 2 kith (1 small, 1 large) overworld + battle poses
- [ ] 1 tactical screen with 3 terrain types, 2 teams, overlays
- [ ] Readability check at ×2 and ×3 scale, and in greyscale
